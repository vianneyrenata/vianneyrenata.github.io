Woops! Peapod has closed for the year.

HFES ASPIRE 2026 · pilot run, Oct 19–25

HFE Peapod was a small experiment in helping conference attendees find the people whose work is closest to theirs. The pilot ended with the conference, and the site is now offline.

Thank you for trying it, claiming your profile, adding your hobbies, and saying hello to a stranger or two. That was the whole point.

Comments, ideas, or a “please bring it back”? Email vrenata@wisc.edu. Fingers crossed for ASPIRE 2027.
